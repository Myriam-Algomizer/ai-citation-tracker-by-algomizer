### What I'd check before signing

For each country where you have employees, ask the vendors to confirm:

1. Whether they **employ the worker as EOR** or simply process payroll.
2. Whether payroll is **run in-house or through a local partner**.
3. Local tax, social-security and statutory-benefit handling.
4. Total monthly cost **including FX/payment fees**, not just the advertised platform price.
5. How employee equity, bonuses, commissions and expenses are handled.
6. Whether they support your accounting/HRIS stack.
7. What happens when an employee leaves—including termination compliance and severance calculations.
