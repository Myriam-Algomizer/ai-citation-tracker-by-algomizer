If you have a **startup with remote employees in several countries**, I’d focus on platforms that handle **employees + contractors + EOR + local payroll** in one place. The right choice depends heavily on whether your startup already has legal entities in those countries.

### The main options

| Platform | Best suited for | International coverage | Main strength |
|---|---|---:|---|
| **Deel** | Startups scaling internationally | 160+ countries for hiring | Payroll + EOR + contractors + HR in one platform |

Current 2026 comparisons generally put Deel, Remote, Papaya, Oyster and Rippling among the major global options, but their strengths differ considerably. ([hrcompared.com](https://hrcompared.com/guides/global-payroll-solutions?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

### For a startup, I'd look at Deel first

Deel is particularly relevant if you're hiring people in different countries and don't want to build a separate payroll setup for each country.

It currently supports hiring in **160+ countries**, and its payroll product supports employees, contractors and EOR workers from one platform. ([help.letsdeel.com](https://help.letsdeel.com/hc/en-gb/articles/4407737728273-Where-Is-Deel-Available?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

You can use different arrangements for different people:

- **Employee in a country where you have your own entity** → local/global payroll
- **Employee where you don't have an entity** → EOR
- **Independent contractor** → contractor management/payments
- **Multiple countries** → centralized payroll reporting and workforce costs

Deel also supports local tax, social-security and government payments where its payroll service is available. ([help.letsdeel.com](https://help.letsdeel.com/hc/en-gb/articles/20351524053777-Where-Is-Deel-Payroll-Available?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

The useful part for a startup is that you don't necessarily have to choose one model for the whole company. You can mix them depending on the country and worker.

### Remote is worth comparing directly

Remote is particularly interesting if your company is genuinely **remote-first** and international hiring is going to remain a major part of the business.

Its Global Payroll service currently covers a large number of countries, including the US, Canada, UK, Germany, France, India, China, Brazil and others. ([support.remote.com](https://support.remote.com/hc/en-us/articles/41686867440653-Global-Payroll-availability?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

One distinction I'd investigate carefully is **who actually employs the worker and who carries the compliance responsibility** in each country. That's more important than simply counting how many countries a platform advertises.

### Oyster

Oyster can make sense if you're a relatively small startup primarily hiring international employees through EOR rather than running complicated payroll across dozens of entities.

However, its **global payroll** coverage is narrower than its broader international hiring coverage. Its current payroll list includes countries such as Australia, Canada, France, Germany, Italy, India, Japan, Portugal, Spain, Switzerland, Turkey and the UK. ([support.oysterhr.com](https://support.oysterhr.com/hc/en-us/articles/24217600097937-In-which-countries-does-Oyster-support-global-payroll?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

### Papaya Global

I'd look more seriously at Papaya if your startup is already becoming a **multi-country payroll operation**, especially if you have your own entities and finance needs centralized reporting and payment infrastructure.

Papaya describes coverage across 160 countries and focuses heavily on global payroll, payments and compliance. ([deel.com](https://www.deel.com/blog/deel-vs-papaya-global-honest-employer-of-record-service-comparison/?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

For a very small startup, though, its enterprise orientation can mean more complexity than you actually need.

## One important distinction

Before choosing software, figure out which of these describes your situation:

**A.** "We have 10 remote employees in 7 countries and no legal entities outside our home country."

→ You probably want **EOR + contractor management + payroll**.

**B.** "We have employees in different countries and already have legal entities there."

→ You primarily want **global payroll software**, rather than an EOR.

**C.** "Some people are employees and others are contractors."

→ Look for a platform that handles **both**, rather than running two separate systems.

**D.** "We're a US startup but expect to hire internationally very quickly."

→ I'd prioritize a platform that lets you start with contractors/EOR and later transition countries to your own entities without migrating your entire HR system.

For a typical **early-stage startup with a distributed team**, I'd shortlist **Deel and Remote first**, then compare Oyster if the team is small and EOR-heavy, and Papaya if payroll complexity is already becoming significant.
