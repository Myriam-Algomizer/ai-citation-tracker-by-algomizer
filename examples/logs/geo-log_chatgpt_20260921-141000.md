### What I'd evaluate before signing

For a startup, don't compare only the headline country count. Check:

- **EOR availability in your exact countries** — especially if you don't have local entities.
- **Employee vs. contractor support** — including worker-classification compliance.
- **Total cost per employee** — EOR fees, payroll fees, FX spreads, benefits and setup fees.
- **Who actually runs payroll** — the vendor itself versus local partners.
- **Local tax/social-security filings and compliance responsibility.**
- **Currency/payment support** and where money is converted.
- **Accounting integrations** with your finance stack.
- **Ease of adding a new country** as the startup grows.

One important distinction: **global payroll software isn't necessarily the same thing as an EOR**. If your startup has no legal entity in, say, France, Germany or Canada, ordinary payroll software doesn't by itself solve the legal-employment problem.
