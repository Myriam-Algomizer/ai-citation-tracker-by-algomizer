### My practical take

**Deel is probably the first platform I'd evaluate for your situation.** Its current offering combines EOR, contractor management and payroll, with payroll available across 130+ countries and broader workforce services in 150+ countries. It also supports both managed and self-service payroll. ([deel.com](https://www.deel.com/en-gb/payroll/global/?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

The important distinction is **where your company has legal entities**:

- **No entity in a country:** You probably want an **EOR**. The provider becomes the legal employer and handles local employment, payroll, taxes and benefits.
- **Your startup already has an entity there:** You generally want **local/global payroll**, rather than paying EOR fees unnecessarily.
- **Mix of employees and contractors:** Choose a platform that handles both and helps with worker classification.
- **Planning to enter many countries:** Country coverage, local compliance expertise and the ability to switch from EOR → your own entity become especially important.

Deel explicitly supports employees, contractors and EOR workers in the same platform, which is useful for a startup whose workforce model may change as it grows. ([deel.com](https://www.deel.com/use-cases/run-global-payroll/?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

### What I'd compare before signing

Don't choose solely on the advertised country count. Ask each vendor for an **all-in monthly cost for your exact countries and employees**, including:

- Employer taxes and statutory contributions
- EOR fees, if applicable
- Benefits/insurance
- Currency conversion and international payment fees
- Payroll implementation/setup
- Year-end tax filings
- Contractor payments
- Offboarding/severance administration
- Accounting integrations
- Support response times
- What happens when you establish your own entity and want to migrate employees off EOR

For a **10–50 person startup with employees in, say, 5–10 countries**, I'd put **Deel, Remote and Oyster** through the same quote/RFP process rather than relying on generic rankings. For a larger organization with complicated existing entities and payroll infrastructure, **Papaya Global** becomes particularly worth investigating.
